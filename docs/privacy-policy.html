Privacy Policy

ChessRadar · Last updated: November 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what ChessRadar collects and how it is used. ChessRadar is an independent Chrome extension and related API. It is not affiliated with Chess.com.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use ChessRadar, we may process:

  • Account data — email, username, and a hashed password if you register
  • Optional profile data — Chess.com username you enter in settings
  • Extension preferences — notification and tool settings stored locally in your browser
  • Chess.com activity used by features — friend usernames, online/playing status, and public game links needed for notifications and watching tools (processed while you use the extension and are logged into Chess.com)
  • Technical logs — basic server logs (e.g. IP, time, errors) for security and reliability

2. What we do not collect

  • We do not ask for your Chess.com password
  • We do not sell your personal data
  • We do not use your data for third-party advertising

3. How we use information

  • Provide login/register and keep you signed in to ChessRadar
  • Show friend activity and send desktop notifications you enable
  • Run optional on-page tools (for example analysis helpers, PiP, time warning)
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, and improve reliability

4. Where data is stored

  • On your device — settings, auth token, and cached friend/status data via Chrome storage
  • On our servers — ChessRadar account records in our database (passwords stored hashed, not plain text)

Chess.com session cookies remain in your browser for Chess.com. ChessRadar uses them only as needed in your browser context to call Chess.com features you use; we do not store your Chess.com password.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share data only with service providers that host our API/database, when required by law, or to protect security and rights.

6. Data retention

Account data is kept while your account exists. You may request deletion of your ChessRadar account by contacting us. Local extension data can be cleared by removing the extension or clearing extension storage.

7. Security

We use reasonable safeguards (hashed passwords, HTTPS for the API in production). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. Children’s privacy

ChessRadar is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your region). Do not create an account if you are under that age.

9. Your choices

  • Disable notifications in extension settings
  • Log out or uninstall the extension
  • Contact us to delete your ChessRadar account

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use means you accept the updated policy.

Privacy questions or deletion requests: support@example.com (replace with your real contact email before publishing).
